Things to try

  1. Start a recording, leave the app, come back. The notification has a stop button.

  2. Tap Camera on a journal entry and take a photo.

    This is the newest change and the one I most want tested.

  3. Download an offline map region, then turn on airplane mode and check the map still works.

  4. Export a track as GPX.

Known issues

Elevation and soil temperature show metric at the moment.

There is no import yet, so if you uninstall, your entries can't be recovered until it gets added.

If something breaks or annoys you, just let me know! Mail support@zynergy-labs.com and it comes straight to me.
