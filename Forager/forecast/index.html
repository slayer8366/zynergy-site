What this is, and what it is not

These are two of the inputs a forecast will use: the soil's acidity and the trees mushrooms grow with. Each square is 250 m. They are shown here so they can be looked at and questioned. They say nothing about this week, and nothing about whether mushrooms are there.

The forecast itself does not exist yet. It will be a weekly sighting chance per mushroom group and weather cell, shown for a region only once it beats a seasonal calendar on years it was not fitted to.

Box: 40 to 49 N, 111 to 125 W. Tree data stops at the border for now: the Canadian side waits for its own source.
