Forager

Forager is a foraging companion with the tools you need for exploring and adventuring outdoors.

Get it on Google Play

OBSERVATIONS

Search iNaturalist observations around your area to see what's been growing, alongside recent rain, soil moisture and the forecast. Whatever you search for shows up as dots on the map. Tap one to see its name, date and location accuracy, then open the observation itself in the iNaturalist app if you have it, or on the web if not.

PRIVACY

Privacy is the reason this project began.
No account, no sign-in. Forager opens straight to the map, and there isn't even an option to make an account.

Your finds stay yours. They aren't part of a community map and are never uploaded. Your finds, tracks, photos and waypoints stay on your device unless you share them yourself.
Photos taken in Forager's camera are stripped of their metadata. A find's location comes from your phone's own GPS, and saving a location is optional.

Back up and restore locally, and choose whether a restore replaces your data or merges with it. You have full control.

Forager handles location itself, with no third-party location library. It weighs GPS against network positions, only lets trustworthy GPS fixes move your track, and blends the barometer with GPS for a steadier altitude. Fuller sensor fusion is on the way.

Offline maps download from Forager's own map server, not a public one. Only the area you download is requested, with no personal information. Once downloaded, the maps work out of signal range.

NAVIGATION

Forager has a full navigation system built on an offline routing engine. It routes along trails, works with no signal, and shows the distance and direction of your next turn as you go.
The Trail Map combines National Forest System trails (USDA Forest Service), Bureau of Land Management trails and OpenStreetMap trail data. It sits between a street map and a topographic map, with relief shading and contours.

Waypoint to waypoint: set several waypoints and Forager routes between each one, with the distance and elevation change for every leg.
Breadcrumb tracks record exactly where you went. The way back takes you to your start along the trails, and if trail data isn't available it follows the path you walked, so it's hard to get lost.

JOURNAL

The journal gathers your photos, tracks, finds, offline maps and waypoints onto a map for each trip. Look back over a whole outing, return next season, or share it easily. Tracks import and export as GPX.

TRIP PLANNING

Trip planning is still growing. Plan a trip to a spot with a date, download the offline maps for that area in one tap, and plot a route through waypoints to see how long and how steep your walk will be.
